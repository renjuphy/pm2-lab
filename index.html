AAbout PM² Lab

PM² Lab develops functional molecular and materials systems through first-principles quantum chemical simulations. Our research enables predictive design of nonlinear optical, optoelectronic, biosensing, and high-energy materials using advanced electronic-structure methods.

  • Density Functional Theory (DFT) & ab initio modeling
  • NLO and optoelectronic materials design
  • Energetic materials and stability prediction
  • 2D nanomaterials for biosensing
  • Gaussian, ORCA, DALTON, Multiwfn workflows

What We Do

RCore Research Areas

QC

Quantum Chemical Modeling

DFT and ab initio simulations for accurate prediction of electronic structure, reactivity, and molecular properties.

NLO

Nonlinear Optical & Optoelectronic Materials

Theory-driven design of high-performance NLO chromophores and functional materials for photonic and optoelectronic applications.

HE

High-Energy & Functional Molecular Systems

Computational design of energetic materials and advanced molecular systems with tailored stability and performance.
